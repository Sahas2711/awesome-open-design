A café menu design example demonstrating Open Design principles with both A4 print format and Instagram story format. The design includes a warm color palette, typography suitable for a café brand, and layout suitable for both print and digital social media use.

## Usage

1. Open this example in your preferred design tool or agent
2. Customize the text and colors to match your café's branding
3. Export as needed for print (A4) or digital use (Instagram story)

## Output Formats

- **A4 Menu**: Full-size printable menu
- **Instagram Story**: Vertical format for social media promotion
